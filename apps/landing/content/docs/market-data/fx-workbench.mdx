# FX Workbench

The **FX Workbench** turns a six-letter currency pair such as `EURUSD` or
`USDJPY` into one read-only research view. It combines price behavior,
country-level macro data, an indicative carry curve, and a manual exposure
scenario without requiring a broker or bank-account connection.

Open **Market**, choose one of the major-pair shortcuts, or search for a
currency pair and open its detail page.

## What the pair view shows

- **Spot and price risk** — the latest close, 1-week, 1-month, and 3-month
  returns, 20-day annualized realized volatility, and the selected range.
- **Price history** — the normal federated K-line chart, including its chosen
  vendor or broker source and freshness metadata.
- **Macro divergence** — base-versus-quote short rates, CPI inflation, and
  OECD leading-indicator momentum.
- **Indicative carry** — 1-, 3-, 6-, and 12-month outright and forward-point
  estimates from covered-interest parity.
- **Exposure scenario** — a local what-if calculation for a long or short base
  currency notional and a move expressed in pips.

The current shortcut set is `EURUSD`, `USDJPY`, `GBPUSD`, and `USDCNH`, but the
pair parser also accepts common spellings such as `EUR/USD`, `USD.CHF`, and
`EURUSD=X`.

## Read the caveats with the numbers

This surface is for research, not execution:

- the latest chart close is a research spot reference, not an executable quote;
- macro observations can have different publication dates;
- EUR uses Germany as its current macro proxy, while CNH uses onshore China;
- indicative forwards use country-level short-rate proxies and omit
  cross-currency basis, bid/ask, holidays, and exact tenor curves;
- scenario P&L is linear spot delta and excludes options convexity, carry,
  funding, fees, and basis.

If one side lacks a short-rate observation, OpenAlice leaves carry blank rather
than guessing. Scenario inputs stay in the browser tab and are never sent to a
broker.

## Where the data comes from

The chart uses the same source-explicit K-line path documented under [Symbol
Search](/docs/market-data/symbol-search#searchbars--federated-k-line-sources).
Macro comparisons use the Data Hub's **global-macro** board, while broad-dollar
context uses the US **macro** board. Every board retains its normal origin,
date, and staleness metadata.
